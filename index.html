01 · Understand the name

What does LLM mean?

L — Large

Large-scale training data, model parameters and computing resources help the model learn complex patterns.

L — Language

The model is designed to process and generate language. Some modern models also work with images, audio and other inputs.

M — Model

A mathematical system whose parameters are adjusted during training to learn patterns from data.

04 · What can LLMs do?

Strengths and limitations

✓ Summarization

Turn a long report into a concise summary or a list of key points.

✓ Drafting

Create a first draft of an email, proposal, or code.

✓ Question answering

Explain complex topics in simpler language.

! Hallucinations

May produce plausible-sounding but false information.

! Knowledge freshness

May lack recent information unless suitable tools or updated data are available.

! Context window

Has limits on how much input and conversation can be processed at once.

Human responsibility

Check facts, calculations, sources, confidentiality, and suitability before using AI output in important work.

Remember: Fluent language does not guarantee truth.

06 · The big takeaway

AI generates.
Humans guide.

LLMs are powerful tools for generating and working with language. Understanding their strengths and limitations helps us use them more responsibly in the enterprise.

From LLMs to Generative AI

LLMs focus on language, while Generative AI also includes systems that create images, audio, video and other content.
